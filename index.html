🚀 My App Architecture

Scroll down to explore the core design mechanics of the interface.

📌

1. The Fixed Top Header & Branding

At the very top sits your fixed navigation bar.
It displays your custom branding text "AYAN" prominently on the left, keeping your identity visible at all times.
On the right side, it holds the sleek hamburger menu button (☰).
This header is styled to stay pinned to the top of the screen
while content slides underneath it,
acting as the anchor for your app's interface.

🚪

2. The Sliding Sidebar Drawer & Dark Overlay

When a user clicks the hamburger button, the app triggers a smooth, sliding drawer layout. The menu transitions gracefully from off-screen (left: -250px) to fully visible, containing clean navigation links to your Home, About, Projects, and Contact pages. Simultaneously, a semi-transparent dark background overlay fades in over your main content,
blurring the background slightly to keep the user focused purely on your menu options.

✨

3. The Scroll-to-Appear Main Content

As users explore your main page content,
elements don't just sit there static—they dynamically wake up.
By utilizing an automatic visibility observer, text blocks, headers, and container boxes stay hidden and slightly lowered by default.
The moment a user scrolls down and an item enters the screen view, it smoothly slides upward and fades in, creating a high-end, interactive narrative feel.

🔔

4. The Smart Scroll-Up Alert Box

If a user decides to change direction and scroll back upwards,
the app responds intelligently.
A custom floating topic box hidden right beneath the header snaps down into view with a playful, springy bounce animation.
This temporary alert card is the perfect spot to pitch specific highlights—like a featured app update, a profile spotlight, or a new project announcement—disappearing quietly the moment they resume scrolling down.

📱

5. The Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar

To maximize responsiveness on smaller mobile devices,
your layout includes a sticky bottom navigation menu.
Complete with distinct icons for Home, Explore, Profile, and Settings,
this bar gives the application a native, thumb-friendly app feel.
It features a distinct "active" state design that instantly tells users exactly
which section of your application they are currently viewing.
